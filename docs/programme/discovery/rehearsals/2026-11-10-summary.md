## Product hypotheses the rehearsals raise

These are candidate changes to test, not decisions.

1. **Assessor verification mode.** Let an assessor re-run a supplier's assessment from its recorded catalogue, tool versions and target hash, independently of the supplier. This is the strongest answer to "self-marking", and may be a separate product for assessors.
2. **Programme view for the MOD side.** A read-only view of several suppliers' gate decisions and coverage, in the same shape for each.
3. **Profile integrity.** Make any change to a profile or a switched-off check visible to the customer, so suppliers cannot quietly tune results.
4. **Fast and slow lanes.** Fast assessors on every pull request; heavy assessments at gates or on a schedule.
5. **Evidence ready before it is asked for.** Position the value as "the pack already exists", not "the pack is faster to assemble".
6. **Contract wording kit.** Model clause text for buyers who want suppliers to use the platform, with open-format evidence and data-return terms.
7. **Decision record for SROs.** A defensible log of who accepted which risk, on what evidence, and when.

## Interview priorities

1. **Cyber security assessors first.** If assessors would not accept platform evidence, the rest of the value falls away. Test A4 and A9 directly with the gate review wireframe.
2. **Supplier security leads second.** They hold the veto (A3) and carry the mapping cost (A1).
3. **One MOD delivery team security lead and one commercial officer** to test the mandate route (A5, A6).
4. **Refresh the DevSecOps engineer persona** with `discovery-persona-builder` from current job adverts; its answers are all inferred.
