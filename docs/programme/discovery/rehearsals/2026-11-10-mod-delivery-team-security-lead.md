# Rehearsal: MOD delivery team security lead

Interviewer: Innovox (rehearsal) · Date: 10 November 2026 · Topic: default script and platform concept

**Walk me through the last time you had to review security evidence for a gate.**
Several suppliers, each sending evidence in their own format. My job is to advise the delivery team leader on security and risk, so I have to turn all of that into one view of where the risks are and whether they're being managed. Most of my time goes on reading and normalising, not on judging risk.

**What took longest? What was redone?**
Chasing gaps. A supplier says a control is in place; I can't tell from what they've sent whether it is. Back and forth until I can.

**What would you need to trust evidence produced by a tool?**
That it maps to Secure by Design the way our assessors understand it, and that the same tool produces comparable evidence across suppliers. Consistency across suppliers would be worth more to me than depth from any one of them.

**Who decides whether a tool like this is used, and how is it bought?**
I can recommend it, and I could push for it to be required in contracts if it made assessment easier. That would need commercial colleagues and the SRO's support. I wouldn't buy it myself.

**What would make you stop using it?**
If evidence ended up somewhere I can't see or control, or outside the boundary the data belongs in. Or if suppliers could tune it to look better than they are.

**Concept: the platform generates evidence continuously from the build and holds gates for a named approver. Where would this fit or fail for you?**
If every supplier used it, I'd get the same shape of evidence from all of them, which is the real prize. Where it fails: I need to know where the data lives and who can see it, and whether a supplier can switch checks off quietly. The approver on the supplier side is fine, but I'd want to see the gate decisions too.

## Objections raised
- How does this map to what our assessors expect?
- Where does the data live, and who can see it?
- Suppliers could tune it to look better than they are

## Assumptions this rehearsal suggests
- Consistent evidence across suppliers is the main value to MOD-side security leads.
- MOD-side leads could drive adoption by requiring it in contracts.
- Visibility of supplier gate decisions is a requirement, not an option.
- Profile tampering by suppliers is a key trust risk to design against.

## Questions to ask real people
- How many suppliers' evidence do you review per gate, and in how many formats?
- Would you want read access to suppliers' assessment results, or only the final pack?
- What would stop you recommending a tool be required in contracts?
